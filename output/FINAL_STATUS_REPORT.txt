🔧 EXTRACTION METHODS EXPLAINED
================================================================================

1. TABLE EXTRACTION (79.5% of records):
   - Identifies structured emission tables in PDFs
   - Parses rows with year, scope, and value patterns
   - Highest confidence when unit is explicit
   - Best for: Annual reports with emission tables

2. SCOPE-SECTION EXTRACTION (10.9% of records):
   - Finds dedicated Scope 1/2/3 sections
   - Extracts emissions from narrative descriptions
   - Uses context to infer missing fields
   - Best for: Detailed sustainability reports

3. STATEMENT EXTRACTION (9.6% of records):
   - Identifies emission declaration sentences
   - Parses natural language statements
   - Requires clear emission indicators
   - Best for: Executive summaries, highlights
